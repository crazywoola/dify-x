# Plugin governance: what we have done · 插件治理：这半年做了什么

A 17-slide review of Dify plugin governance from May to November 2026, written for a non-technical audience (the CMO Office bi-weekly of 24 November 2026). It covers what was built, what the data shows about demand, how other ecosystems signal trust, how DeepSeek Harness runs its own plugin ecosystem, and a proposal to run governance as Dify’s trust and partner channel — the first bricks of a trust layer.

## Evidence boundaries

- Slide 03 paraphrases an internal discussion. It contains no direct quotations and it is anonymised: no speaker, team or role is named, and the slide presents the questions raised rather than any claim about what leadership wants.
- Slide 14 is a proposal by Community Ops. Nothing on it is scoped, staffed or agreed.
- Slide 17 makes no ask and sets no threshold. It is a statement plus five observations, each traceable to an earlier slide, and it names what we are watching next.
- Slide 13 compares ecosystem design with DeepSeek Harness, a public agent runtime. A harness plugin installs into a local profile and a Dify plugin runs as a hosted service; the slide makes no like-for-like product claim.
- Slide 15 frames the same internal figures used elsewhere as four returns — brand, distinctive features, data, efficiency. No outside revenue or growth claim is made.
- "1 in 8" on slide 04 is derived (160 new plugins ÷ 1,278 total).
- Figures on slides 05 and 09 were counted from GitHub on 24 November 2026 and will drift.
- "Vendor-published" on slide 10 is a heuristic (author name appears in the plugin name), not a census.
- The twelve systems on slide 11 were chosen by Community Ops for large Japanese companies; SAP and ServiceNow are there by judgement, without a verified Japan customer count, and the Japan team has not reviewed the list.
- Outside figures (GitHub, WordPress.org, Perforce, CISA, Atlassian, Salesforce, Cybozu) are quoted from the pages linked in `research.md`; they are context, not evidence about Dify.
- The Phase 1 targets on slide 06 have not been measured against a baseline.
